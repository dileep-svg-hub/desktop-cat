Cat sounds (optional)
=====================

Desktop Cat is silent unless you add sounds here. Put files named:

  meow.wav   (or meow.mp3 / meow.ogg)   - played when the cat meows (every few minutes)
  purr.wav   (or purr.mp3 / purr.ogg)   - played when you click or pet the cat

Keep them short (meow under 1 second, purr 1-2 seconds) and soft.
Restart the app after adding or changing a file. Once there are sounds,
right-click the cat to find "Mute sounds".

Only use sounds you recorded yourself or that are free to share (e.g. CC0 from freesound.org).
